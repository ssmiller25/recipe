Creamy, cheesy hash brown potatoes made right in the crock pot — a hands-off Thanksgiving side that stays warm on the buffet. Frozen pre-shredded hash browns keep prep to a minimum, and using the slow cooker leaves the oven free for the turkey.

## Ingredients

- 1 (30 oz) bag frozen pre-shredded hash browns, thawed
- 1 (10.5 oz) can condensed cream of chicken soup (**sub:** cream of mushroom to keep it vegetarian)
- 1 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted
- 2 cups (8 oz) shredded sharp cheddar cheese, divided
- 1/2 cup finely diced yellow onion (optional)
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- Optional topping: 1 cup crushed cornflakes or buttered breadcrumbs

## Instructions

1. Prep the crock pot
    1. Coat the inside of a 4- to 6-quart slow cooker with nonstick spray.
    1. Make sure the hash browns are fully thawed (see Notes).
2. Mix the sauce
    1. In a large bowl, whisk together the soup, sour cream, melted butter, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until smooth.
    1. Stir in the diced onion and 1 1/2 cups of the cheddar.
3. Combine
    1. Add the hash browns to the bowl and stir until evenly coated.
    1. Transfer to the slow cooker and spread into an even layer.
4. Cook
    1. Cover and cook on LOW for 4-6 hours (or HIGH for 2-3 hours), until hot and bubbly and the cheese is fully melted.
    1. Stir once halfway through if you're around; it isn't required.
5. Finish and serve
    1. Sprinkle the remaining 1/2 cup cheddar over the top, cover, and cook 10-15 minutes more until melted.
    1. Turn the slow cooker to WARM to hold until ready to serve. Sprinkle with crushed cornflakes or breadcrumbs just before serving, if using.

## Make-Ahead

- Assemble the night before: mix and transfer to the slow cooker insert, cover, and refrigerate overnight. Start it in the morning on LOW and add 30-60 minutes to the cook time if it goes in cold.

## Tips & Substitutions

- For a crispier top, the crock pot won't brown crumbs on its own — spoon a portion into an oven-safe dish and broil 2-3 minutes, or just pass the crushed cornflakes at the table.
- Add a pinch of paprika or a couple of slices of cooked, crumbled bacon for a smoky version.
- Sharp cheddar gives the most flavor; a cheddar-Jack blend also works.

## Source/Story

Drafted for Thanksgiving 2026 as a hands-off side. Based on the classic cheesy hash brown (funeral potato) casserole, adapted so everything comes together in the crock pot instead of the oven.

## Notes

- Thaw the hash browns overnight in the refrigerator — not on the counter — for food safety, or use the microwave's defrost setting if you're short on time.
- Cook times are for a 4- to 6-quart slow cooker; a smaller cooker may run hotter and finish sooner, so start checking at the low end of the range.
- Leftovers keep in the refrigerator for up to 4 days and reheat well in the microwave or on LOW in the slow cooker.
